# Protocol Synopsis — Study ST-004 (NCT09000004)

**Title:** A Phase 2, Randomised, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study of Neurolynt (neurolyntamab) in Early Alzheimer's Disease
**Sponsor:** Nordlicht Therapeutics
**Compound:** Neurolynt (CPD-104), an anti-amyloid-beta monoclonal antibody
**Phase:** 2  |  **Therapeutic area:** Neurology  |  **Planned enrollment:** 140 subjects  |  **Status:** Recruiting

## Objectives
- Primary: change from baseline in CDR-SB (Clinical Dementia Rating - Sum of Boxes) at Week 24.
- Secondary: amyloid PET change, ADAS-Cog, safety.

## Design
Subjects with confirmed amyloid pathology are randomised 1:1 to Placebo (ST-004-A1) or Neurolynt 10 mg/kg intravenously every 4 weeks (ST-004-A2). Nine sites in the United States, United Kingdom, Netherlands and Canada.

## Safety monitoring
**Amyloid related imaging abnormalities (ARIA)** are the key identified risk of anti-amyloid antibodies. MRI is performed before dosing at Weeks 4, 12 and 24. ARIA-E (oedema) and ARIA-H (haemorrhage) events are reported as adverse events with preferred term "Amyloid related imaging abnormalities". Symptomatic or severe ARIA requires dose interruption. Headache and confusional state are also expected. Subjects aged 65 and over carry higher ARIA risk.
