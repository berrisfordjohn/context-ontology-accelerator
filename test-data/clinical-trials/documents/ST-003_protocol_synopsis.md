# Protocol Synopsis — Study ST-003 (NCT09000003)

**Title:** A Phase 3, Randomised, Double-Blind Cardiovascular Outcomes Study of Cardiprex (cardiprexin) in Adults with Heart Failure
**Sponsor:** Nordlicht Therapeutics
**Compound:** Cardiprex (CPD-103), an SGLT2 inhibitor
**Phase:** 3  |  **Therapeutic area:** Cardiology  |  **Planned enrollment:** 220 subjects

## Objectives
- Primary: change from baseline in NT-proBNP (pg/mL) at Week 24 for Cardiprex 10 mg versus placebo.
- Secondary: hospitalisation for heart failure, NYHA class change, renal function (creatinine), safety.

## Design
Subjects are randomised 1:1 to Placebo (ST-003-A1) or Cardiprex 10 mg once daily (ST-003-A2) on top of standard heart failure therapy. Fourteen sites in the United States, Germany, Sweden, Brazil, the United Kingdom and Canada.

## Safety monitoring
Adverse events of special interest for the SGLT2 class are **genital mycotic infections**, volume depletion and hypotension, and diabetic ketoacidosis. Serum creatinine is monitored at every visit; a small early rise in creatinine (up to about 10%) is expected with SGLT2 inhibition (haemodynamic effect) and is not by itself a reason to discontinue.

## Relationship to other studies
Cardiprex was first studied in the Phase 1 single-ascending-dose study ST-005 in healthy volunteers (5, 10 and 20 mg). The 10 mg dose used in ST-003 was selected from ST-005 pharmacodynamic data (urinary glucose excretion).
