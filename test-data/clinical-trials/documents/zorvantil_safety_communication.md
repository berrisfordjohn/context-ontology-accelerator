# Safety Communication — Zorvantil (zorvantinib) JAK1 inhibitor

**From:** Helvetia Biopharma Pharmacovigilance
**Applies to:** Study ST-001 and any future Zorvantil study

## Summary
Review of blinded and unblinded data for Zorvantil identified two risks that investigators must monitor:

1. **Herpes zoster.** As with other JAK inhibitors, Zorvantil is associated with reactivation of herpes zoster. Events were observed in both Zorvantil dose groups and the risk appears higher at the 10 mg dose than at 5 mg. Subjects should be counselled to report painful rash promptly. Vaccination against zoster is recommended before starting treatment where permitted by local practice.
2. **Hepatic enzyme elevation.** Increases in alanine aminotransferase (ALT) above the upper limit of normal (40 U/L) have been seen more often with Zorvantil than with placebo, and the proportion rises with treatment duration. ALT above 120 U/L (3x ULN) must be repeated within 72 hours.

## Required actions
- Review ALT/AST at Week 4, Week 12 and Week 24 (all scheduled visits).
- Interrupt study drug for confirmed ALT above 3x ULN together with symptoms, or above 5x ULN regardless of symptoms.
- Report every serious infection and every case of herpes zoster as an adverse event with MedDRA preferred term "Herpes zoster".

## Benefit-risk
In ST-001, both Zorvantil doses reduced DAS28-CRP from baseline to Week 24 more than placebo, with a larger reduction at 10 mg. The DSMB recommended continuing the study without modification.
