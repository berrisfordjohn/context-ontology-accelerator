# Protocol Synopsis — Study ST-001 (NCT09000001)

**Title:** A Phase 3, Randomised, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study of Zorvantil (zorvantinib) in Adults with Moderate-to-Severe Rheumatoid Arthritis
**Sponsor:** Helvetia Biopharma
**Compound:** Zorvantil (CPD-101), a selective JAK1 inhibitor
**Phase:** 3  |  **Therapeutic area:** Immunology  |  **Planned enrollment:** 160 subjects

## Objectives
- Primary: demonstrate superiority of Zorvantil 5 mg and 10 mg once daily over placebo on change from baseline in DAS28-CRP at Week 24.
- Secondary: ACR20/50/70 response, HAQ-DI, and safety and tolerability.

## Design
Subjects are randomised 1:1:1 to three arms: Placebo (ST-001-A1), Zorvantil 5 mg (ST-001-A2) and Zorvantil 10 mg (ST-001-A3). Treatment lasts 24 weeks with laboratory assessments at Screening, Week 4, Week 12 and Week 24. Efficacy (DAS28-CRP) is assessed at Baseline, Week 12 and Week 24.

## Key eligibility
- Age 18 years or older with active rheumatoid arthritis despite methotrexate.
- Inadequate response to at least one conventional DMARD.
- Exclusion: active tuberculosis, history of herpes zoster within 12 months, ALT or AST above 2x the upper limit of normal at screening, estimated GFR below 40.

## Safety monitoring
Because Zorvantil is a JAK inhibitor, the protocol requires special attention to **herpes zoster**, serious infections, and **liver enzyme elevations (ALT/AST)**. An independent Data Safety Monitoring Board (DSMB) reviews unblinded safety data every 3 months. Any ALT result above 3x the upper limit of normal (120 U/L) must be repeated within 72 hours; confirmed elevations trigger study-drug interruption.

## Sites
Ten sites across Germany, Poland, the United Kingdom, Spain and the United States.

## Statistical considerations
The primary analysis uses a mixed model for repeated measures on the full analysis set. Multiplicity across the two dose levels is controlled with a hierarchical testing procedure.
