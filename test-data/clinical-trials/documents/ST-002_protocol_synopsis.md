# Protocol Synopsis — Study ST-002 (NCT09000002)

**Title:** A Phase 2, Randomised, Open-Label Study of Oncotra (oncotrazumab) versus Docetaxel in Previously Treated PD-L1 Positive Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer
**Sponsor:** Helvetia Biopharma
**Compound:** Oncotra (CPD-102), an anti-PD-L1 antibody; comparator Docetaxel (CPD-900)
**Phase:** 2  |  **Therapeutic area:** Oncology  |  **Planned enrollment:** 90 subjects

## Objectives
- Primary: compare change from baseline in the sum of target lesion diameters (mm) at Week 24.
- Secondary: objective response rate, progression-free survival, overall survival, safety.

## Design
Subjects are randomised 1:1 to Oncotra 1200 mg every 3 weeks (ST-002-A1) or Docetaxel 75 mg/m2 every 3 weeks (ST-002-A2). Tumour assessments occur at Baseline, Week 12 and Week 24.

## Key eligibility
- Histologically confirmed stage IIIB/IV NSCLC with PD-L1 tumour proportion score of 50% or higher.
- Progression after one platinum-based regimen. ECOG performance status 0-1.
- Exclusion: active autoimmune disease requiring systemic therapy, untreated brain metastases.

## Safety monitoring
The comparator Docetaxel is expected to cause **neutropenia**; absolute neutrophil count (ANC) is monitored at every visit and Grade 3-4 neutropenia is managed per the dose-modification table. For Oncotra, immune-related adverse events (rash, colitis, hepatitis, pneumonitis) are of special interest. Rash and fatigue are the most common events expected with Oncotra.

## Sites
Seven sites across Germany, France, Japan and the United States. Study status: active, not recruiting.
